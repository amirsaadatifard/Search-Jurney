A studio, not a factory

{{ placeholder("The workshop on a firing day", 150, "4 / 3") }}

We opened in 2012 with one second-hand kiln and a rented garage. Today three of us work in a bright corner building, but the method has not changed: wedge, throw, trim, glaze, fire, and check every piece by hand.

We fire twice a week. A kiln load holds about ninety mugs, and roughly one in ten does not pass inspection. Those seconds are sold at the studio door at half price, because a small glaze flaw does not stop a mug from holding coffee.
