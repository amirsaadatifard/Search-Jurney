About the studio

Three people, two kilns and a shared belief that the things you use every day deserve care.

{{ site_name }} is a small ceramics studio. We make functional stoneware and teach others how to make their own. Nothing leaves the building without being picked up, turned over and checked by one of us.

We keep the range deliberately short. A dozen shapes, refined over years, are more useful than a hundred made once. When a design stops improving, we stop changing it.

How we work

We buy clay from a single regional supplier and recycle every trimming. Failed pieces are crushed and reused as grog, which gives our clay body its slightly speckled look. Packaging is paper and cardboard only.

Workshops are capped at eight people so that everyone gets time on a wheel. Beginners are welcome; most of our students have never touched clay before.
